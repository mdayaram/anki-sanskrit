internal_consonant_sandhi:natva:brahman+ā	<center><big><big><big>ब्रह्मन् + आ</big></big></big></center>	<center><big><big><b>ब्रह्मणा</b></big></big><br><big>brahman + ā → brahmaṇā</big><br><br><b>न् becomes ण् after र् ऋ ॠ or ष् in the same word</b><br>Three things must all hold: (1) a trigger — र् ऋ ॠ or ष् — earlier in the same word; (2) the न् is followed by a vowel or by न् म् य् व्; (3) only vowels, क-varga, प-varga, य् व् ह् or anusvāra stand in between. Any other intervening sound — a palatal, cerebral, dental, ल् श् or स् — blocks it.<br><br><small>trigger र् · through अ ह् म् (ह् + प-varga) · न् before आ</small></center>

internal_consonant_sandhi:natva:car+anti	<center><big><big><big>चर् + अन्ति</big></big></big></center>	<center><big><big><b>चरन्ति</b></big></big><br><big>car + anti → caranti</big><br><br><b>Blocked: the न् must be followed by a vowel or by न् म् य् व्</b><br>Compare चर् + अन → चरण. The trigger र् and the intervening अ are the same in both; here the न् is followed by त्, which is not one of the permitted following sounds, so it stays dental. This is also why a word-final न् never retroflexes — nothing follows it.<br><br><small>trigger र् · through अ (vowel) · न् before त् — condition 2 FAILS</small></center>
internal_consonant_sandhi:natva:arjuna+ena	<center><big><big><big>अर्जुन + एन</big></big></big></center>	<center><big><big><b>अर्जुनेन</b></big></big><br><big>arjuna + ena → arjunena</big><br><br><b>Blocked: an intervening palatal, cerebral or dental stops ṇatva</b><br>The र् of अर्- would retroflex the न् of the ending, but the palatal ज् stands between them and is not one of the permitted intervening sounds — hence अर्जुनेन, never अर्जुणेन. Compare राम + एन → रामेण, where only the labial म् intervenes and does not block.<br><br><small>trigger र् · through ज् (palatal) — condition 3 FAILS</small></center>
