mantra:pratah_smarana	<center><big><big>प्रातः स्मरामि हृदि संस्फुरदात्मतत्त्वं<br>सच्चित्सुखं परमहंसगतिं तुरीयम्<br>यत्स्वप्नजागरसुषुप्तिमवैति नित्यं<br>तद्ब्रह्म निष्कलमहं न च भूतसङ्घः<br><br>प्रातर्भजामि मनसा वचसामगम्यं<br>वाचो विभान्ति निखिला यदनुग्रहेण<br>यन्नेतिनेतिवचनैर्निगमा अवोचं<br>स्तं देवदेवमजमच्युतमाहुरग्र्यम्<br><br>प्रातर्नमामि तमसः परमर्कवर्णं<br>पूर्णं सनातनपदं पुरुषोत्तमाख्यम्<br>यस्मिन्निदं जगदशेषमशेषमूर्तौ<br>रज्ज्वां भुजङ्गम इव प्रतिभासितं वै</big></big></center>	<b>Translation — Swami Tadatmananda (Arsha Bodha):</b><br>At dawn, I meditate on the reality shining in my heart,<br>sat chit ananda, sought by saints, transcendent,<br>which forever abides in waking, dreams, and sleep.<br>I am that indivisible brahman, not this material body.<br><br>At dawn, I worship him beyond words and thoughts,<br>who causes all words and thoughts to arise,<br>declared as "not this, not this" in the scriptures,<br>the unborn, immutable, primal, god of gods.<br><br>At dawn, I bow to him shining through the darkness,<br>the limitless, eternal abode, the supreme person,<br>in whom the entire world appears<br>like a snake appears on a rope.<br><br>[sound:pratah_smarana.mp3]
