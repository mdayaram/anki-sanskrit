case_example:sukham-jivati	<center><big><big><big>सः अतीव धार्मिकः भूमिपः <b>सुखं</b> जीवति</big></big></big><br><big>That very righteous king lives <u>happily</u>.</big><br><small>Which case, and which use?</small></center>	<center><big><big><big><b>Nominative (प्रथमा)</b></big></big></big><br><br><b>1e</b> The neuter nominative singular can work as an indeclinable adverb: सुखम् “happily”<br><br><big><b>सुखं</b> sukhaṃ</big> ← सुख sukha “happiness; as an adverb, “happily””<br>neut. nominative singular<br><br>saḥ atīva dhārmikaḥ bhūmipaḥ sukhaṃ jīvati<br><br><small>सुखम् is the neuter singular, identical in the nominative and the accusative.</small><br><br><small>source: Goldman reading, Lesson 4</small></center>
