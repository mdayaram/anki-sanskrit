internal_consonant_sandhi_split:satva:vi+sīdati	<center><big><big><big>विषीदति</big></big></big></center>	<center><big><big><b>वि + सीदति</b></big></big><br><big>viṣīdati → vi + sīdati</big><br><br><b>स् becomes ष् after any vowel except अ/आ, or after क् or र्</b><br>Three things must hold: (1) the sound just before the स् is a vowel other than अ/आ, or क-varga, र् or ल्; (2) the स् is neither word-final nor followed by र् (so तिस्रः and तमिस्र keep their स्); (3) only anusvāra, visarga or a sibilant may intervene. Note the contrast with ṇatva, which reaches across vowels, क-varga, प-varga, य् व् and ह् — here the trigger must otherwise be immediately adjacent. हिंस् and पुंस् are lexical exceptions.<br><br><small>trigger इ (a vowel other than अ/आ) · nothing intervening</small></center>
